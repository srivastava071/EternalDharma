🌼 About Eternal Dharma

“The light of Dharma is eternal — it shines in every age, in every heart that seeks truth.”

Eternal Dharma is a humble digital sanctuary created to preserve and share the timeless wisdom of Sanatana Dharma — through the sacred stories of the Ramayana and Mahabharata.

This platform is designed not just for reading, but for *experiencing* — where ancient values meet modern design, and every visitor can journey through the path of truth, courage, and compassion.

Here, simplicity meets spirituality. Every page has been carefully crafted with love and devotion — to remind us that Dharma is not just in scriptures, but in how we live every moment.

🪔 Created by Guru Himanshu Srivastava

A student of Computer Science and an admirer of Sanatana philosophy, Himanshu blends technology and tradition to build something meaningful.
Through Eternal Dharma, he wishes to bring the essence of Dharma to everyone — in a way that is modern, clear, and inspiring.

“Code with devotion. Create with purpose. Share with light.”
